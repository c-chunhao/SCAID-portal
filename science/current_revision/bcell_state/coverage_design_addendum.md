# Source-label coverage addendum

Coverage diagnostics were specified after the initial source-label review; they
are not an external preregistration. Individuals with B cells but none of the
three named state labels have unavailable state-composition outcomes rather than
three observed zeros. Other/unassigned B cells remain in all-B denominators.
Individual identifiers and batch-level tables are excluded from this package.

The additional coverage models examine missing, other-labelled and total
unresolved fractions. A >=90% named-state-coverage sensitivity uses the same
source selection, >=20 total B cells for fractions, >=20 state cells for IFN,
>=10 eligible individuals per arm and the same full-rank/HC3 checks. Planned
unavailable tests enter their BH family as P=1 and remain displayed unavailable.
These are overlapping within-source checks, not independent validation.
